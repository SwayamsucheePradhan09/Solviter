Frequently Asked Questions

Is SOLVITER free to use? ×

Yes, SOLVITER is completely free for students, with no hidden charges.

What facilities can I access through SOLVITER? ×

You can check daily hostel menus, and find facilities like laundry, mall, medical, parks, PGs, rentals, salon, tailor, bakery and bookstore — all in one place.

Does SOLVITER work offline? ×

No, you’ll need an internet connection to view the latest updates and access features.

Can I get notifications for important updates? ×

Yes, SOLVITER provides notification alerts for menus, events, and other important updates about college.

Can I suggest new features or report issues? ×

Absolutely! There’s a “Report/Contact Us” section where you can submit feedback and ideas.

Does SOLVITER only work for my hostel/college? ×

SOLVITER is designed for student communities and can be adapted for multiple hostels and our college.
